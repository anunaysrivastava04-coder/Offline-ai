# 🔗 Important Links & Resources

This document contains all the necessary URLs required to download the engine and models for the portable offline AI setup. 

### 1. Llamafile Engine
Llamafile is the core engine that allows the AI models to run on your local computer without any complex installation.
* **Mozilla AI llamafile v0.10.6 (Release Page):**
  [https://github.com/mozilla-ai/llamafile/releases/tag/0.10.6](https://github.com/mozilla-ai/llamafile/releases/tag/0.10.6)
  *(Download the Windows `.exe` file from this page to place on your pendrive).*

### 2. Recommended AI Models (GGUF Format)
You will need to download a model in the `.gguf` format to act as the "brain" for your AI. Below are the recommended models for this setup:

* **Qwen3 4B Thinking Finetune:**
  [https://huggingface.co/pramodlohra/Qween3_4B_thinking_finetune](https://huggingface.co/pramodlohra/Qween3_4B_thinking_finetune)
  *(A lightweight, fine-tuned model great for machines with limited RAM. Look for files ending in `.gguf`, such as `qwen3-4b-thinking-2507.Q4_K_M.gguf`).*

* **Qwen3 8B GGUF Models:**
  [https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-8B-GGUF/tree/main](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-8B-GGUF/tree/main)
  *(A larger, more capable model. Recommended for computers with at least 16 GB of RAM).*

---
**Tip:** Always ensure you are downloading the models in the `.gguf` format, as this is the format compatible with the Llamafile setup described in this project.
